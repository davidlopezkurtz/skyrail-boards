MUSTER WARDEN costs 3 marks, adds one Rail Warden, caps at 4, and is never

stake is the haul committed to the run: provisions and toll, spent at the
click, refunded in no direction, on either branch. A turned-back run pays
ZERO (CFD-145); the engine's x0.25 consolation leg is refused, not copied.

Nothing on this board moves with wall time: wait() stands, takes nothing, and

Every run ends in a sentence and so does the sitting. A turned-back run says
all four of the beat's clauses — zero pay, stake spent and returned in no
direction, everyone who rode is home, the desk stands — and it names whoever
actually rode, so a run with no Wardens aboard promises no crew home. The
terminal reads the record in the beat's two registers, keyed on whether every
run the player sent came home (runsTurnedBack), NOT on what the turn-backs
cost. Keying it on cost made "Clean record" reachable over a sitting with any
number of turned-back FREE halt runs — thirteen out, four banked, nine turned
back, called clean. The paid register names the turned-back count and then the
cost, naming a zero cost rather than omitting it. NEITHER register names the
weather: the board prints three failure agents and only Cloud Basin's is
weather, so a terminal that blamed weather would misread its own record on the
two Core Line routes. The ledger spells its counts at any magnitude, because
the free floor send is unlimited and a table that stopped at twenty would drop
a bare numeral into a clause of spelled words; marks stay figures.

MUSTER is a ladder of four ABSOLUTE berths - berth k means roster = k - on the
desk's own furniture ground. Row 1 never dims: a control that fades when it
cannot be paid reads as chrome, and one that shows the price you cannot yet
pay reads as an instruction. Berths are 44px, because the control they replace
was 34px against a 52px card and a 76px pad - the only control on the board
under the touch floor, and the measured cause of a maximally affordable muster
being walked past twice at 10 and 22 marks. One press-and-release recruits up
to the berth chosen, ALL OR NOTHING, with the total REPLACING the price on the
face before any commit. Release-to-commit is deliberate: nothing here refunds,
so sliding off the ladder is an escape a tap would not have had. Tracking
lives on window and setPointerCapture is optional, so the gesture always
terminates and no provisional number can be left standing with no finger down.
Verified in a browser at 375x812 under mobile emulation with real
PointerEvents: press, slide, release off (no commit), release on (commits
exactly), pointercancel, window blur, and a second finger correctly ignored.

While a berth is armed every card re-quotes at that roster through the sim's
one chanceFor(), asked for as cards(atRoster) and never computed on the board.
The provisional number is typed apart on three axes at once - its own colour,
italic, and a leading arrow a committed number never carries - and it dies
with the gesture on every path out, in the same frame. At rest and while away
every card shows chanceFor(baseRisk, roster) and nothing else.

Layout: nothing may escape its box, at any viewport. The priority under
pressure is scenery first, then the sentence panels (which yield and scroll
inside a 46% cap), and the cards last — cards never shrink below their own
