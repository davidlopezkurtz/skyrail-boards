Two endings, two sentences, and they do not share one - David,
2026-09-04: "the player can't tell whether the sitting ended because
the wallet emptied or because a rule fired." They share a fiction, the
larder is empty, and not a cause. Both land on the consist, the tile
the player tapped to get there. endSentence is never rendered.

PRESS_COST is REPAIRED, not improved. The parent's "The larder covered

Roll her out and there is nothing left to collect." - true on both
outcomes and from the instant she is out.

coordinated overwrite of both copies. Self-pinning is part of recording
a pass, and four boards have now needed it on the day they passed.
