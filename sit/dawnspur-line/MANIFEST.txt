THE ONE NEW SYSTEM IS THE JOIN: a send's provisions come off the terrace.
Provisions 0 / 2 / 3 do not move; only their unit does, from marks to FOOD.
The Chartered toll stays 1 and stays MARKS, because a toll is a civic fee and
R1 names it as one. NOT ONE FIGURE MOVES - 3 / 2 / 3+1 / 10 / 14 / 18 / 0.036
/ UP 3,4,5 / reserve depth 4 / roster cap 4 all stand.

Food is not a currency, and every clause is a Kill line this board honours:
food buys exactly one thing (the provisions leg of a send); no commit trades
it in either direction; it has one source, the carry; it is never a HUD
figure; and it never pays a toll, a muster or an UP. Marks rise in exactly one
place in the sim (a route's payout) and food in exactly one (the carry), and
the tests pin both counts. R1 is satisfied literally: marks and food never
share a sink.

CARRY pays FOOD, not marks, and that was forced rather than chosen. With CARRY
paying marks and provisions drawn off the ground, TEND-then-CARRY mints
(level - 1) marks per two turns with the ground unchanged and no ceiling. So
the terrace pays goods and the line pays money.

The stores hold 6, and 6 is DERIVED in the sim rather than typed: a carry
strands whenever cap - stores < level, the player carries only when
stores < provisions, so a carry never strands iff cap >= maxProvisions +
maxLevel - 1 = 3 + 4 - 1. At cap 4 or 5 the ladder's top rung buys NOTHING on
the only route that fires the ending - cap 4 and cap 5 are the same board.
Measured against the sim, carries per Chartered send: 3.000 / 1.500 / 1.000 /
0.750 by level at cap 6, and 3.000 / 1.500 / 1.000 / 1.000 at cap 4 and 5.

TWO INHERITED VERBS ARE CUT, both RULED by David 2026-08-27, both because the
join changed what they were for. MOSSWAKE +3: the join put a ROUTE of that
name on this board, so the verb was redundant - "cutting redundant is cheaper
than renaming colliding". Its precondition is the two-vehicle claim and this
board honours it: no terrace verb reads the away state, so CARRY and UP stand
beside MEET while a run is out, verified in a browser. TEND: the join gave its
job to the food line and its reckoning is weather, which deferred to the storm
sitting; it measured DOMINATED, not merely inert. It appears nowhere as a
verb, a field or a path, and it returns on the storm board.

The reserve is ONE-WAY ON THIS SITTING, BECAUSE TEND IS ABSENT FROM IT - not
as a property of the reserve. It is two-way again when TEND returns. In the
sim it is DERIVED from the carry count rather than stored, which makes
"written by anything but a carry" impossible by construction.

The record carries TWO loss counters and the terminal reads both, naming a
zero rather than omitting it. Mosswake's toll is 0, so a turned-back Mosswake
run loses food and NO marks, and a cost clause keyed on marks alone would have
closed the sitting with "cost nothing but the trip" over dead food - the exact
defect CFD-196 was sent back for, re-opened by the join in a new currency.

Topping the terrace ARMS the ending and says so out loud; the next Chartered
cargo home FIRES it. A Chartered cargo banked while unarmed is a good run and
nothing more; a turned-back one while armed is not an ending. So the sitting
cannot end without the terrace twice over - level 4 arms it and three food
from the ground fires it.

The terminal's ground clause has TWO registers and not three. The full
register was cut because it CANNOT FIRE: the stop needs a Chartered outfit of
3 food, the only source of food is CARRY, and every CARRY draws a step, so
carries >= 1 at every stop. Re-measured here by breadth-first search over the
whole reachable space: the reserve at a stop is 0, 1, 2 or 3 and never 4.
That search also re-earns the claim CFD-196 Amendment 1 had to retire - every
state reached from the opening by play - which is what opening the stores at 0
buys: reserve 4 with stores > 0 is not reachable, so minting a store would
have put the board off its own lattice a second time.
