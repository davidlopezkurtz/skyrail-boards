THE ONE NEW SYSTEM IS WEATHER. Three sky states, one step per committed
player action. Cycle fixed: five clear, two bird, two storm, period nine.
wait() still exists, takes nothing, returns false, and does not advance the
sky. The bird is news, not weather. Storm: every sendable route -0.10;
Chartered pay 18->24; Core Line pay unmoved. Sky advances AFTER the commit
resolves; a turn resolved under storm then draws the ground one step.

The Ranger (2 marks, cap 1) is the weather unit. TRIM is a second send on
the same card, only in a storm, only with a Ranger: clear chance, clear pay,
the route's own stake plus two marks. runsTrimmed increments only on a
committed trimmed send and the terminal reports it as a RATE against storm
sends.

The reserve is STORED, not derived. Exactly three writers: carry -1, storm
-1, tend +1. In a storm CARRY pays min(level, storesCap - stores, reserve).
In clear and under the bird it pays the level whenever the stores can hold
it, at every reserve, bare included.
